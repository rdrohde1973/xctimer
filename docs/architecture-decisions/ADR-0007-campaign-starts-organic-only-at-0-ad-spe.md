# ADR-0007: Campaign starts organic-only at $0 ad spend

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-05
- **Cites:** Q-0002, WP-0004

## Context

Rob answered Q-0002: $0 to start, organic only, then decide.

## Decision

This supersedes provisional ADR-0002 and confirms it. Phase 1 is organic only: the Facebook Page, posts, and helpful participation in coach/AD/race-director Groups. No paid ads. The paid test plan stays in the playbook as a future option that Rob turns on (T4).

## Consequences

The ads deliverable (WP-0004) puts the organic calendar first. Paid variants are drafted but marked "for later paid phase". Pixel/CAPI is still worth installing because it builds retargeting audiences for later.
