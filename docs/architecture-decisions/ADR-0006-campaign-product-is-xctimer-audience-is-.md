# ADR-0006: Campaign product is XCTimer; audience is coaches, ADs, districts and fun-run organizers

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-05
- **Cites:** Q-0001, WP-0003, WP-0004, WP-0005

## Context

Rob answered Q-0001: XCTimer, the junior high/middle school XC and track meet manager. The brief's contractor/draw-request audience is dropped. Today XCTimer charges a one-time $50 fun-run fee through Square, with no subscriptions and no self-serve coach signup. It's November 2026: XC season is ending and spring track is the next season.

## Decision

This supersedes provisional ADR-0001. All product-specific work markets XCTimer to four segments: (1) junior high/middle school XC and track coaches and meet hosts, (2) athletic directors, (3) district administrators, (4) community fun-run/5K organizers. Ads target adults in professional and interest audiences only, never minors, consistent with Meta policy. Messaging is timed to spring track (Jan–May) and next fall's XC. The goal is free-tier signups that convert to monthly paid plans. Plan structure and prices stay PROVISIONAL(Q-0003). Construction and draw-request content is out of scope.

## Consequences

WP-0003/4/5 are unblocked and rewritten for these segments. WP-0005 must flag that XCTimer has no self-serve coach signup or subscription billing; that gap becomes later T2 code WPs. The SPEC is updated.
