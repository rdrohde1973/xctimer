# ADR-0010: Rob has a Meta Business account and Facebook Page; ad account deferred

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-05
- **Cites:** Q-0004, WP-0007

## Context

Rob answered Q-0004: he has a Business account and a Page but probably no ad account. Phase 1 is organic only at $0 (ADR-0007).

## Decision

This supersedes provisional ADR-0004. Organic launch uses Rob's existing Page and Business account. Creating an ad account is deferred until Rob chooses to start paid ads, and stays a Rob-only (T4) step. The playbook checklist is reordered: verify the existing assets first (Page name, profile and cover, CTA button "Sign Up" pointing to the landing URL; domain verification of xctimer.com in the Business account), then optional ad account and Pixel steps for later.

## Consequences

The Pixel can still be created under the Business account without an ad account. The playbook needs a small edit (WP-0007).
