# ADR-0008: Provisional: For the 30-day XCTimer trial: what's the monthly price after it ends, and should

- **Status:** provisional
- **Date:** 2026-11-05
- **Cites:** Q-0005

## Context

You chose "Free 30-day trial, then $X/mo" but didn't give the price. Landing page copy and the billing build both need it. Card-up-front gets fewer signups but better conversion to paid; no card gets more trials. A single price per school or team keeps it simple.

## Decision

Until the owner answers Q-0005, the team proceeds with: One plan per school at a placeholder price, PROVISIONAL(Q-0005); no card required to start the trial.

## Consequences

Revisit when the owner answers. Never promoted to accepted by an agent.
